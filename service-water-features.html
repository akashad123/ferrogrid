Quick answer

How are artificial waterfalls and rock grottos made?

Artificial waterfalls and rock grottos are built in ferrocement: a steel frame and wire mesh set the shape, then cement mortar is applied and hand-sculpted to look like natural rock. The basins are seamless and watertight, so water features hold water and weather naturally over time.

Overview

Nature, sculpted by hand

Ferrocement can be shaped and textured to look like natural rock, which makes it ideal for waterfalls, grottos, ponds and fountains. Every feature is sculpted by hand on site.

We also create artistic rock work — carved grotto entrances, cave interiors, landscaped steps and feature walls — that become signature spots in resorts and gardens.

FAQ

Questions about water features

More answers in our ferrocement FAQ, or contact our team — we respond within 24 hours.

Will a ferrocement waterfall look natural?

Yes. Our craftsmen sculpt and texture the mortar by hand to look like natural rock, matched to your landscape.

Can a water feature be added to an existing resort or pool?

Usually, yes. We assess the site, water supply and drainage during the site visit and design the feature to fit.

How much does a waterfall or grotto cost?

There is no fixed price — every project is priced individually. The cost depends on your land (slope, soil and access), the type and size of the structure, and the finishes you choose. Share your site details for a free consultation and a quote.

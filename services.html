FAQ

Choosing the right service

Can't find your answer? Contact our team — we respond within 24 hours.

Which service is right for my property?

Tell us about your site, your guests and your budget during the free consultation and we will suggest the best options — often a combination, such as cottages with a pool or a water feature.

Do you work outside Kerala?

Yes. We currently build across Kerala, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, and we are expanding pan-India and internationally. Get in touch wherever your site is.

Can you combine several services in one project?

Yes. Our Kakkadampoyil project combines earth-sheltered cottages, dome houses and a swimming pool on one site, built by the same team.

Quick answer

What farm buildings can be built in ferrocement?

Ferrocement suits most farm and plantation buildings: farm stay guest cottages, vaulted cattle sheds, water storage tanks, rainwater harvesting systems and biogas plants. Its dense, mesh-reinforced shells stand up to monsoon rain and heat, need little maintenance and are economical to build.

FAQ

Questions about farm stay buildings

More answers in our ferrocement FAQ, or contact our team — we respond within 24 hours.

Can you build cattle sheds and farm structures?

Yes. We build vaulted ferrocement cattle sheds with arched ventilation windows and easy-to-clean floors, along with water storage tanks and biogas plants.

Are ferrocement buildings suitable for heavy monsoon areas?

Yes. Dense ferrocement shells are weather-resistant, and we plan roof shapes, drainage and finishes for local rainfall.

How much does a ferrocement farm building cost?

There is no fixed price — every project is priced individually. The cost depends on your land (slope, soil and access), the type and size of the structure, and the finishes you choose. Share your site details for a free consultation and a quote.

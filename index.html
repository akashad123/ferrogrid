Who We Are

Specialized Ferrocement Construction Company

We are a specialized ferrocement construction company providing innovative, durable, and cost-effective construction solutions for residential, commercial, and customized projects.

We responsibly undertake a wide range of ferro-cement works inside and outside Kerala for resorts and homestays, including ferro-cement underground cave houses, underground DJ/multipurpose auditoriums, mud-packed eco-houses, pillar houses, A-frame and semi-A-frame structures, treehouses, swimming pools, pool villas, artistic works, designed compound walls, water storage tanks, rainwater harvesting systems, and biogas plants.

Our expertise in ferrocement construction allows us to create lightweight, economical, and flexible designs in almost any shape or structure according to customer requirements. We focus on quality workmanship, modern construction techniques, affordability, and sustainable building solutions while delivering reliable structures.
